## LifeCalc

Ok so we all know how it feels to have isues counting your age or 
even how many days its been since u did something or the period of time it took for something
 so I have made Lifecalc a calculator thsat helps you count severel things such as bmi,intreast,tip, discount, percenbtage or just convert units

 ## What can it do?

 It can calculate several things such as 
 
 - Your age
 - Date ( How many days and weeks it has in that time period )
  - Tip ( Basedon the percentage and bill u gave )
- Discount ( Basedon the percentage and bill u gave )
- Percentage( X percent of Y )
- BMI ( Based on your hieght and weight )
- Interest calculator ( Based on the rate of interest, principle, time and type of the interest)
- Unit converter ( So you can convert units easily )
